The reported UART wiring matches the firmware pin assignments below. UART must
be crossed (ESP32 RX to STM32 TX and ESP32 TX to STM32 RX), with a shared ground.
The ESP32 GPIOs use 3.3V logic and are not 5V tolerant. The 3V3 rail connection
is recorded as reported; ensure the boards' power sources are compatible and do
not tie together competing regulator outputs.

6. ELECTRICAL CHARACTERISTICS & CONNECTION GUIDELINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Signal Levels: Strictly 3.3V CMOS logic. Never connect 5V TTL logic directly to GPIO 16, 17, 4, or 5.
- Trace Lengths: Keep UART lines (GPIO 16 <-> PA9, GPIO 17 <-> PA10) under 20 cm.
  If running between separated enclosures, twist pair with GND or use RS-485 level shifters.
- Reset Isolation: GPIO 5 MUST operate in open-drain mode (`pinMode(5, OUTPUT_OPEN_DRAIN)`) or remain in INPUT
  mode when idle so it does not pull STM32 NRST line low during brownouts.
- Decoupling: Ensure 100uF electrolytic + 100nF ceramic capacitors are installed near the ESP32 5V/VIN header.
