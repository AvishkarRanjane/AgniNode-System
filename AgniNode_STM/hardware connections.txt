Target MCU: STMicroelectronics STM32G0B1RET6 (ARM Cortex-M0+ @ 64 MHz)
Memory: 512 KB Flash | 144 KB SRAM | 12-bit 2.5 MSPS SAR ADC
Operating Logic: 3.3V DC (I/O pins 5V-tolerant in digital mode)

The low-voltage Vbus monitor is not connected. Firmware reports it unavailable and
inhibits automatic mains SSR close; manual close remains available. The conditioned
ZMPT101B signal must be biased and remain within 0-3.3V at PA0; its mains calibration
must be verified using appropriate isolated test equipment before mains operation.

1. ANALOG SENSOR INPUTS (12-BIT ADC1 VIA TIM3 DMA CIRCULAR SCAN @ 12.8 kHz)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STM32 Pin   ADC Channel   Sensor Component        Input Range       Circuit / Calibration Notes
---------   -----------   ----------------        -----------       ---------------------------
PA0         ADC_IN0       ZMPT101B AC Voltage     0 - 3.3V Biased   Conditioned mains-voltage waveform input.
                                                                    Must be biased to the ADC midpoint and
                                                                    calibrated before mains use.

PA4         ADC_IN4       MQ Smoke Sensor (A0)    0 - 3.3V Analog   Smoke chamber analog concentration.
                                                                    Alarm ON threshold: >= 2200 counts.
                                                                    Alarm Clear hysteresis: < 1900 counts.

PA5         ADC_IN5       Line CT (SCT-013-030A)  0 - 3.3V Biased   Line Current (I_L) sensor. 30A = 1V RMS.
                                                                    Biased to Vref/2 (1.65V) with 10uF filter.
                                                                    Scanned for RMS, Arc Fault & RCD analysis.

PA7         ADC_IN7       Neutral CT (SCT-013)    0 - 3.3V Biased   Neutral Current (I_N) sensor. 30A = 1V RMS.
                                                                    Biased to Vref/2 (1.65V) with 10uF filter.
                                                                    Used for differential Earth Leakage (RCD).

---------   -----------   ----------------        ------------      ---------------------

3. SAFETY ACTUATORS, INTERLOCKS & AUDIBLE ALARMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STM32 Pin   Function      Actuator Device         Active State      Safety / Interlock Invariant
---------   -----------   ---------------         ------------      ----------------------------
PB0         GPIO Output   Solid State Relay CH1   Active HIGH (3.3V) Aerosol Suppression Igniter.
                          (Suppression Igniter)                     Pulled LOW on boot. Fires aerosol canister
                                                                    upon confirmed multi-sensor fire event.

PB1         GPIO Output   Solid State Relay CH2   HIGH = CLOSED     Mains Power Interlock / Disconnect.
                          (Mains AC Breaker SSR)  LOW  = OPEN (TRIP) Fail-safe: Boot default is LOW (OPEN).
                                                                    Automatic close inhibited while the
                                                                    Vbus monitor is absent.
                                                                    Trips OPEN instantly on Fire, Arc Fault,
                                                                    or Residual Shock / Earth Leakage (>300mA).

PA6         GPIO Output   Audible Piezo Buzzer    Active HIGH (3.3V) Push-Pull output. Drives local transistor/
                                                                    MOSFET buzzer stage. Pulses on warning,
                                                                    continuous sound on alarm / fire latch.

7. HARDWARE SAFETY PROTOCOL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Never alter PB0 (Aerosol Igniter) or PB1 (Mains SSR) routing without full isolation testing.
- High-voltage AC (110-240V) connects EXCLUSIVELY to ZMPT101B primary and SSR contact terminals.
  Maintain minimum 6.3 mm creepage and clearance to all MCU signal traces.
- Current transformers SCT-013-030A feature internal burden resistors with 1V RMS output at 30A.
  Do NOT use bare-current (mA output) CTs without calculating and installing external burdens.
