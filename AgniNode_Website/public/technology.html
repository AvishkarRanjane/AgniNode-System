Safety decisions must never wait on Wi-Fi handshake timeouts or cloud server availability. AgniNode separates the real-time deterministic safety loop from high-level connectivity, executing arc analysis in hardware on every single AC cycle.

Why conventional breakers cannot detect an arc.

A standard Miniature Circuit Breaker (MCB) operates on two simple physical principles: a bimetallic strip that bends on slow thermal overload, and an electromagnetic solenoid that trips on massive short-circuit currents.

An electrical arc fault is neither: it is an intermittent plasma discharge caused by loose screw terminals, oxidized busbars, or rodent-chewed cables. The current draw is often only 5A to 12A — completely normal to a 16A or 32A breaker — but the plasma reaches 3,000°C to 6,000°C, igniting panel plastics in minutes.

AgniNode's Spectral Solution:

The high-bandwidth current sensing front-end passes the line current through an active analog filter into an ultra-fast 200ksps conversion pipeline. Deterministic firmware scores the broadband 20–100 kHz frequency energy burst that is the unmistakable signature of arcing, differentiating it from normal resistive loads.

Multi-Sensor Fusion & Cross-Validation

Single-trigger detectors lead to dangerous false trips. AgniNode correlates electrical, thermal, optical, and chemical signals together.

Arc Spectral Burst (Primary)

High-frequency energy thresholding flags arcing hours before heat develops. Detects both series (loose connection) and parallel (phase-to-phase) arcs.

Sampling: 200ksps • Band: 20–100 kHz

Rate-of-Rise Thermal (dT/dt)

Mounted adjacent to terminal busbars, precision solid-state thermal sensors track absolute temperature and sudden temperature slope (dT/dt), catching thermal runaways early.

Accuracy: ±0.3°C • Resolution: 0.01°C

Optical Photoelectric Smoke

Dual-beam optical smoke chamber is specifically tuned to detect large, slow particulate smoke released from smouldering PVC, XLPE, and rubber wire insulation.

Chamber: IR LED + Photodiode Scatter

Infrared Flame Confirmation

Broad-angle infrared phototransistor monitors interior enclosure reflections. Serves as an undeniable confirmation signal for instant aerosol suppression release.

Range: ~1m inside distribution board
