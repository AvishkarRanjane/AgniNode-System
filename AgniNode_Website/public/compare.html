Why in-line AFDDs have failed to penetrate India.

In Europe and North America where AFDDs are legally mandated, they are built directly into the circuit breaker itself. To adopt them in India, a homeowner or factory must:

  • 1. Throw Away Existing Breakers: Discard functional MCBs from trusted brands like L&T, Havells, or Polycab.
  • 2. Completely Rebuild the Switchboard: Certified AFDDs require different busbar combs and double the module width, making them impossible to fit inside standard Indian consumer units without tearing down walls.
  • 3. Pay 5x to 8x Cost Multipliers: Imported AFDD units retail for ₹18,000 to ₹35,000 per circuit.

Retrofit Beside, Not Inside.

AgniNode mounts on the same standard 35mm DIN rail next to whatever breaker you already own. It snaps its split-core current transformer non-invasively around the live conductor and attaches two wires to the breaker's shunt-trip accessory.
