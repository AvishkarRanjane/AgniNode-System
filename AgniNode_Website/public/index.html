Standard breakers can't see what's about to burn.

Conventional MCBs trip on overcurrent. RCCBs trip on earth leakage. Neither can detect the intermittent 20–100 kHz micro-arcs that smoulder for hours inside switchboards before bursting into flames.

The Fatal Engineering Blindspot

When loose connections or degraded cable insulation cause micro-arcing, the current remains well within the normal operating load. A 16A breaker sees nothing abnormal while copper vaporizes at over 3,000°C.

See AgniNode in Action

Watch the actual working hardware prototype detect electrical anomalies, actuate local protection, and communicate telemetry in real-time.

1. High-Speed Current & Voltage Sampling

Non-invasive split-core current clamping and isolated line voltage sensing capture waveform transients at up to 200ksps through an active anti-aliasing front end.

2. Autonomous Hardware Shunt-Trip

Upon threshold confirmation, an isolated solid-state relay contact energizes the breaker's shunt-trip coil, cutting panel power instantly without cloud latency.

3. In-Panel Aerosol Suppression

If armed with the physical key switch, an autonomous high-current actuator triggers the condensed aerosol cartridge, extinguishing interior flames within 2 seconds.

How AgniNode Protects in Milliseconds

Safety decisions never depend on Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or cloud round-trips. Protection is executed strictly on-device.

1
SENSE
High-bandwidth current and voltage sensors sample electrical lines alongside optical smoke, IR flame, and precision temp/humidity sensors.
2
ANALYZE
Autonomous Safety Core runs high-frequency spectral scoring and rate-of-rise (dT/dt) analysis locally on every 50Hz cycle.
3
DECIDE
Multi-sensor cross-validation rules out false positives (e.g. power tool surges vs real sustained arcing).
4
ACT
Sounds 85dB piezo alarm, fires circuit breaker shunt-trip, and releases aerosol suppression if key-switch armed.
5
INFORM
Dedicated Telemetry Engine pushes event logs and real-time frames to the cloud dashboard and mobile app via Wi-Fi/RS-485.
RAPID PANEL RETROFIT

Installed in minutes beside your existing breakers.

Unlike European or American in-line AFDDs from Schneider or Siemens that require replacing your entire consumer unit and re-engineering busbars, AgniNode clips directly onto standard 35mm DIN rails.

  • Non-invasive split-core current transformer clamping
  • Standard MX/SD shunt-trip coil interface for MCBs & MCCBs
  • Auxiliary (OF) feedback contact to verify physical breaker opening
  • 24V DC auxiliary input with LiFePO4 battery backup for power-fail alerts
