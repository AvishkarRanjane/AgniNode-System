AgniNode Hardware Architecture

Complete physical breakdown of the AgniNode DIN-rail fire-prevention module: flame-retardant industrial enclosure, front-panel controls, audio/visual indicators, and panel terminal connections.

Enclosure, Controls & Interface Specifications

Engineering specifications of the external housing, front-panel controls, field interfaces, and connection terminals illustrated in the 3D model above.

Front Panel Controls & HMI

Bi-colour Status LED

Dual-state front optical window. Steady green indicates system armed, normal line voltage, and zero arc activity. Flashing red indicates micro-arc detection, sensor threshold breach, or active trip condition.

Acoustic Alarm Sounder

High-decibel acoustic sounder aperture (≥85dB at 10cm) delivering continuous audible alert tones during confirmed arc-fault signatures and in-panel thermal runaway events.

Physical ARM / Key-Switch

Two-position mechanical lock-switch with removable physical key. Serves as a deterministic safety interlock (ARM / OFF) preventing inadvertent aerosol discharge or shunt tripping during board servicing.

Tool-Accessible Service Hatch

Front-accessible maintenance compartment secured by a central machine screw. Permits rapid field inspection and certified cartridge replacement without removing the module from the DIN rail.

Enclosure & Industrial Connectivity

Industrial RS-485 Fieldbus Interface

Differential half-duplex RS-485 communication interface allowing multi-panel daisy chaining and Modbus RTU communication with building management systems (BMS) and industrial PLCs.

35mm Top-Hat DIN Rail Mounting

Rear universal snap-fit mounting mechanism engineered for EN 50022 standard 35mm top-hat DIN rails. Allows zero-modification retrofit directly adjacent to existing standard circuit breakers.

Flame-Retardant Polycarbonate Enclosure

High-durability self-extinguishing housing rated UL94 V-0, providing robust mechanical protection and IP40 environmental sealing for harsh electrical distribution boards.

Factory Calibration & Service Port

Tamper-evident service port reserved exclusively for certified factory diagnostics, calibration checks, and authorized safety firmware verification.

Bottom Terminal Block Connections

LINE IN (L / N)

Universal mains power input with integrated line surge suppression and wide-range regulation (operating range 85V–264V AC / 24V DC).

CT SENSE

Precision analog sensing input for external split-core current transformers. Samples load current and broadband 20–100 kHz arc spectral signatures.

V SENSE

Galvanically isolated line voltage feedback for real-time phase tracking, over/undervoltage monitoring, and power factor analysis.

SHUNT TRIP & AUX CONTACT

SHUNT TRIP: Optically-isolated solid-state trip contact that energizes the external breaker shunt coil in <5ms.
AUX CONTACT: Supervisory feedback loop confirming breaker contacts have physically separated.
